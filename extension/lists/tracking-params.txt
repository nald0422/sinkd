! Signed URLs: their signature covers the query string, so removing a
! parameter breaks the link. These exceptions cancel parameter stripping
! only, never blocking.

@@*X-Amz-Credential=$removeparam
@@*X-Goog-Signature=$removeparam
@@*Key-Pair-Id=$removeparam
@@*&sig=*&se=$removeparam
@@*&se=*&sig=$removeparam
